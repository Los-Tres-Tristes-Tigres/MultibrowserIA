# Orbit video identity

## Style Prompt

Orbit is a precise, human-centered control room for browser agents: a deep navy-black field, luminous orbital blue, clear cyan transfers, selective amber approval states, and reassuring green completion states. The video should feel technically confident and legible rather than neon-noisy. Use a spacious editorial grid, measured motion, and interface-like details that make the workflow intelligible at a glance.

## Colors

- `#101418` — canvas
- `#E9EEF5` — primary copy
- `#1676F8` — action / orbital blue
- `#32C7FA` — context transfer
- `#F2C453` — approval attention
- `#5DE39A` — completed / safe state

## Typography

- `DM Mono` for system labels and workflow metadata
- `Fraunces` for product statements

## What NOT to Do

- Do not introduce gradients, purple, or generic cyberpunk effects.
- Do not make every element glow or float at the center.
- Do not represent autonomous browser actions as bypassing human approval.
- Do not use tiny web-sized text or a dense dashboard screenshot as the only focal point.
